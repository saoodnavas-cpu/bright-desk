About us

Brightdesk Foundation started in 2016 when a group of retired engineers and school teachers noticed the same thing: donated computers sat locked in cupboards because nobody had time to learn them.

So we changed the model. We don't drop off equipment. We pair every classroom with a trained teacher, a repair plan, and a mentor who visits each term.

Our mission: make digital learning an ordinary part of every school day, in every language the children speak.

  • Teachers firstA computer helps only when the person in front of the class is confident with it.
  • Refurbish, don't discardEvery machine we install is a donated laptop or desktop given a second life.
  • Stay long enoughWe measure our work after three years, not on installation day.
  • Local languagesAll lessons and guides are written with local teachers in the medium they teach.

Services

Four programs that work together. Schools can join one or all.

Digital classroom setup

Refurbished computers, power backup and a simple lab layout, installed and tested by our technicians.

Teacher training

A 12-week course taught in short evening sessions, ending with each teacher running a real lesson.

Student skill clubs

Weekly clubs for ages 11 to 16 covering typing, safe internet use, basic coding and making presentations.

Community learning hours

Evening and weekend access for parents and youth to learn forms, banking apps and job applications.
